# SOFiSTiK member results

Graviss reads scalar diagrams from the documented SOFiSTiK DATEN records listed below. The source provides the quantity names, groups, physical units, display conversion and drawing direction; the viewer does not contain a fixed SOFiSTiK quantity list.

## Available results

| DATEN key        | Quantity family                         | Projection                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 102/LC, BEAM_FOR | Total beam forces                       | N, Vy, Vz, Mt, My, Mz, warping bimoment Mb, secondary torsion Mt2 and tertiary torsion Mt3. Primary torsion Mt1 is Mt − Mt2 − Mt3 when all three terms exist. These total forces include plate contributions to T-beams.                                                                                                               |
| 102/LC, BEAM_FOR | Local deformations                      | ux, uy, uz, rotations phix, phiy, phiz and twisting phiw. Stored displacements use metres, rotations radians and twisting inverse metres.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 102/LC, BEAM_FOR | Pile and beam bedding                   | Axial bedding pa, transverse resultant pt, and transverse components pty and ptz. DATEN quantity 1095 stores kN/m, converted to N/m; these are distributed forces, not pressures.                                                                                                                                                      |
| 112/LC, BEAM_FTR | Beam forces without plate contributions | A distinct force group with IDs prefixed `beamOnly:`. It is never added to the total forces or substituted for them. Optional unavailable torsion terms remain missing.                                                                                                                                                                |
| 103/LC, BEAM_STI | Section stiffness                       | EA, GAy, GAz, coupled GAyz, GIt, EIy, EIz, coupled EIyz and the linear EIy/EIz values. Axial and shear stiffnesses use N; bending and torsional stiffnesses use N·m².                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 103/LC, BEAM_STI | Section state                           | Inner N, Vy, Vz, Mt, My, Mz and Mb; total axial strain; minimum/maximum fibre and reinforcement strains; plastic and total curvatures; current crack opening; elastic-centre eccentricities; shear-centre offsets; primary axial force; and plastic shear/torsion factors. IDs use the `section:` prefix.                              |
| 105/LC, BEAM_STR | Section stresses                        | Minimum/maximum normal stress, shear stress, equivalent stress, principal tensile/compressive stress, upper/lower fibre stress, normal/shear stress range, weld stress, bond force, shear reduction factor, partial-section axial force and decompression stress.                                                                      |
| 105/LC, BEAM_STR | Section strain and position             | Partial-section centre strain, strain gradients in y/z, and partial-section centre coordinates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 105/LC, BEAM_STT | Individual tendons                      | Tendon stress and stress range, qualified by tendon number.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 111/LC, BEAM_HRC | Implicit hinges                         | N, Vy, Vz, Mt, My and Mz reactions; total hinge deformation; plastic compression/tension deformation for PLAS/PISO/HYPE laws; the documented extrema and inner-loop state for MTAK/PIVO laws; and performance indices for categories 0–5. Each is a point sample, so separate hinges are never joined into a continuous force diagram. |

Stress curves are qualified by material, reinforcement, tendon material, named stress point or shear cut. Their values are never averaged together. Quantities that DATEN explicitly leaves undefined for a category, such as upper/lower fibre and weld stresses for reinforcement or tendons, remain missing. All fields with dimensions are converted from their own documented storage quantity to SI; display units such as kN, kN·m, MPa, mm and mrad are supplied separately.

The thrust line is derived relative to the elastic centre as y = −Mz/N and z = My/N, with the resultant distance √(y² + z²). The reference-axis group adds the actual ys/zs offsets from 103/LC. A zero axial force has no finite thrust-line distance. The diagram is broken at stored zeros and at a zero crossing within the sampled axial-force segment, rather than joining through an asymptote. Reference-axis values remain unavailable when the source has no centre offsets.

## Station and availability rules

The coordinate x is measured from the member start in metres. Negative beam numbers identify the left bank at a discontinuity, positive numbers identify the right bank, and a zero continues the current element. The two coincident stations retain their order and values. Different materials, stress points and hinge types retain separate quantities.

Short records are supported only at documented boundaries or through the reader's explicitly reported documented force prefix. A missing deformation, warping, bedding or stress tail is represented by `null`, even if the reader's shared typed column contains zero padding. A stored zero remains zero. Unknown declared storage units and malformed records produce an error instead of a guessed quantity.

The source merges the station grids of the selected case. When another family adds an intermediate station, a continuous quantity receives its existing piecewise-linear ordinate there. This does not extrapolate beyond the original sampled range or interpolate across missing values. Point quantities remain defined only at their actual stations. Intermediate beam-moment or displacement extrema that SOFiSTiK Graphics constructs with additional interpolation are not presented as solver-written samples.

Result discovery includes cases carrying any of the supported member families, even when they contain no nodal displacements. Selecting Displacement for a case with neither nodal nor member deformation results reports that the field is unavailable. Changing the member quantity reuses the loaded case matrix.

## Other DATEN result domains

DATEN contains substantially more than scalar force diagrams. The following records are not read into the current member-diagram matrix:

| DATEN family                         | Data and required interpretation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 103/LC, remaining BEAM_STI fields    | Solver iteration increments, warping stiffness parameters and legacy fields whose dimensional semantics differ from their printed label. They are not inferred from adjacent quantities.                                                                                          |
| 104/LC                               | Creep/shrinkage interval parameters, imposed and total forces per material, changes in tendon force, and thermal eigenstress profiles. These require material and construction-history interpretation; they are not silently added to total beam forces.                          |
| 105/LC BEAM_TST and 1105/LC BEAM_CST | Thermal stress profiles through the section and composite element/construction-stage strain descriptions. Their additional section coordinates and stage identities require a section-result domain.                                                                              |
| 106/DC                               | Reinforcement totals and individual longitudinal/transverse layers, including design-error codes and shift-rule information. DC identifies a design case, which must not be confused with LC.                                                                                     |
| 107/LC and 1107/LC                   | Ultimate/plastic section design results and composite utilisation checks, including check identities and design diagnostics. These are not currently projected as member quantities.                                                                                              |
| 108/LC                               | Pile-group stiffness and bedding reduction factors. These are whole-element factors rather than station force records.                                                                                                                                                            |
| 109/LC                               | Internal load patterns, imposed deformations, prestress and curvature loading. These are loads and solver input state, not section-result diagrams.                                                                                                                               |
| 115/LC                               | Nonlinear material-point stresses, strains, crack opening, temperature and performance data, identified by position within the cross-section.                                                                                                                                     |
| 116/LC                               | Tendon forces, duct/tendon compensation, strain difference, eccentricity, age and accumulated relaxation, qualified by tendon identity.                                                                                                                                           |
| 122–147 and related families         | Design lines and external sections have their own topology and result identities; they are not remapped to physical beam IDs.                                                                                                                                                     |
| STAR2 INFL load cases                | Influence coefficients are stored in displacement-shaped records. Their response normalization and global load direction are required before assigning units. Member-diagram requests reject these cases explicitly rather than presenting influence coefficients as millimetres. |
| ELLA influence databases             | The separate `.cdi` database stores location and lane data in IL_CTRL/IL_DATA records. Its abscissa follows a traffic lane, not a finite member.                                                                                                                                  |

The record definitions and storage units were checked against the installed SOFiSTiK DATEN headers and CDBASE documentation: explicit/implicit units (`cdbase125`/`126`), beam forces (`174`), stiffness (`175`), eigenstresses (`176`), stresses (`177`), reinforcement (`178`), design (`179`), pile reductions (`180`), hinges (`203`), material points (`210`), influence data (`212`) and tendon results (`242`). The ASE manual §2.3.3 defines total torsion as primary + secondary + tertiary; the STAR2 manual §2.2 defines local force signs, and §3.5 describes INFL coefficients. The Graphics manual's display-unit labels are not used as storage-unit definitions.
